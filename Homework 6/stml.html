The Story

I'm unlucky. Especially in forests. For whatever reason. Now that additional unluckiness wouldn't matter, I mean how often would I wonder into a forest? Except I'm currently in the forest. And I'm scared. Why would I enter the forest when I know that it makes me more unlucky? I don't remember. This morning, I ate something, then I ended up here. I have no clue what I ate, I could've been poisoned for all I know, but I definately ate something. Whatever that food item did to my brain made me think that it was a great idea to enter the woods, and now I'm here, lost. Real unlucky huh? Now that being said, nothing unlucky has happened since then. But that's exactly why I'm scared! Unluckiness strikes suddenly, and it sucks. For all I know, a bear could run out suddenly and maul me to death. I don't even live in an area with bears! But what if! I'm going to lose my mind here.

Whatever, that's enough moping. I decide to start moving again after reflecting upon my life. It's hard to describe where I'm currently located in this forest. There's just trees upon trees upon trees upon trees, it's a little suffocating. And of course the sky had to be overcast today so I'm getting no moral support from the sun. Earlier on my "expedition," I stumbled upon a random trail and I have been following it ever since. I hope it's a human trail, but what if it's bears? I don't really have any other choice but to follow it though. I continue to treck through the forest on this lone path hoping for something to appear. Trees. Trees. Trees. Bees. Oh no.. I pick up the pace a bit until I feel safe to cruise again. I'm so tired.

After walking what felt like multible hours, I finally emerge into a clearing. A bunch of mushrooms? More than a bunch. An ecosystem. The sheer amount of mushrooms was more than I have ever seen in my entire life. This is overwhelming. I wonder further into the mushroom room and then freeze dead in my tracks. There was a sound. I wait. Nothing. What the hell. Suddenly, I see round ears poke out from a bush. Oh my god. I fall on the ground in a panic. Then, just as suddenly as the "bear" appeared, all of my hairs stood on their ends. Oh my god the bear is going to eat me. Boom. Lightning struck. Right on me. I got struck by lightning.

Great story amiright.
